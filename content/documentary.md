## Morgan's Wave

Assistant to the director and stills photographer on Morgan's Wave, a feature documentary directed by Flavio Graziano and written by Claudio Orlandi for Ombrosa World. Shot across Rome, Tuscany, and Sardinia over three months in 2022, the film follows a young surfer with a physical disability through his return to the water. I worked on-location through the production: coordinating logistics across three regions, managing schedules and equipment between the crew and contributors  and shooting stills throughout.
